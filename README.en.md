## ⚽ About

**National Football Manager** (*«Сборная: Футбольный Менеджер»*) is a national team manager in the spirit of the genre's classics. Take charge of any of 210 national teams, call up players, pick your tactics, play matches in a minute-by-minute engine and lead your country through qualifiers, Nations Leagues, continental championships and the World Cup. Between international windows there is the FIFA ranking, draws, federation finances, stadiums, hosting bids and news from all over the world.

The game runs right in your browser on phone or desktop — no sign-up, and no internet needed after the first load. Windows and Android builds are available too.

> ℹ️ All player, coach and club names are fictional and generated. Any resemblance to real people is coincidental.

## ✨ Features

### 🌍 The football world
- 210 national teams from 6 confederations, Elo and star strength ratings
- FIFA ranking using the SUM formula: monthly snapshots, movers, biggest climbers and fallers, history back to 1993
- Every competition: 48-team World Cup, Euro, Copa América, AFCON, Asian Cup, Gold Cup, UEFA and CONCACAF Nations Leagues, play-offs, Olympics, youth World Cups and Euros
- Every match in the world is simulated by the same engine — live tables, brackets and top scorers

### 🎲 Draws and tournaments
- Animated draws with pots based on the FIFA ranking
- Confederation rule in every intercontinental tournament
- Hosting bids, voting, co-hosts and World Cup rotation

### ⚽ Matches and tactics
- Minute-by-minute engine: possession, shots, xG, cards, VAR, weather, stoppage time
- 2D pitch, momentum graph, commentary, pause and ×4 speed
- In-game subs and tactics, extra time and penalty shoot-outs, player ratings
- Formations, team and individual instructions, drag & drop line-ups
- v10: 13 formations, player roles with suitability, instruction sliders, set pieces, 7 one-tap styles, conditional instructions and auto-subs, A/B/C tactic slots, opposition instructions, tactical familiarity
- v10: interactive FIFA ranking (changes, search, filters, team chart, compare), settings screen (difficulty, injuries, fatigue, weather, currency, units, font size); careers start on 1 January 2027
- v10.1: an «Auto» button on the squad, tactics and training screens that explains its choice in plain words; saves about a third smaller, seasons simulate ~20% faster

### 👥 Your squad
- Call up 18–23 players with 7 skills, form, morale, potential and personality
- Training per line or per player with an Auto mode, training camps
- U-21 team, academy, dual nationality and naturalisation
- Press conferences, player talks, federation trust — and the sack

### 💰 Federation and economy
- Sponsors, TV, tickets, prize money and merchandise versus costs
- Build and expand stadiums in different cities
- Kit sponsor deals and a home/away kit designer
- Friendlies with fee negotiations

### 📊 Stats and news
- Shot maps with xG, heatmaps, player comparison radar
- World stats: top scorers, records, streaks, team strength
- A world results digest after every window, club news, Hall of Fame

### 🏆 Career and rivalries
- ~60 derbies plus rivalries born in the knockouts: head-to-head, press headlines, higher stakes
- Coaching career: reputation, contract, job offers, resigning and finding a new national team
- 44 achievements and 12 scenarios with a goal and deadline; tips for newcomers

### 🎨 Comfort
- 20 UI languages, including RTL (Hebrew, Arabic, Persian)
- 5 themes: Stadium Night, Classic Pitch, Midnight Blue, Light, Sand/Retro
- 20 save slots + autosave, export/import, fully offline
- Optional cheats (the save gets flagged)

## 🚀 How to play and install

| | |
|---|---|
| 🌐 **Browser** | Just open the game page — it works on phone and desktop, and you can “Add to Home Screen” → [https://danyadanileckiy.github.io/national-football-manager/](https://danyadanileckiy.github.io/national-football-manager/) |
| 🪟 **Windows** | Download the portable file and run it — no installation needed: [`NationalFootballManager-Windows-portable.exe`](https://github.com/DanyaDanileckiy/national-football-manager/releases/latest/download/NationalFootballManager-Windows-portable.exe) |
| 🤖 **Android** | Download and install the APK: [`NationalFootballManager-Android.apk`](https://github.com/DanyaDanileckiy/national-football-manager/releases/latest/download/NationalFootballManager-Android.apk) |
| 💻 **Locally** | Clone the repository and open `index.html`, or run `python3 -m http.server` |

> ⚠️ **Windows SmartScreen:** the file isn't code-signed, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Click **“More info” → “Run anyway”**.
>
> ⚠️ **Android:** to install the APK, allow **“Install unknown apps”** for your browser or file manager (Settings → Security).

💾 Saves are stored locally on your device (IndexedDB). To move a career between devices, use export and import in the saves menu.

## 🛠️ Tech

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — **no build step, no frameworks, no dependencies**
- A static site on GitHub Pages that works fully offline
- Saves in IndexedDB with gzip compression; the calendar is simulated in small chunks so the UI never freezes
- i18n: 20 languages, country names via `Intl.DisplayNames`, translation sources in `i18n/src`
- Windows build — Electron (portable), Android — Capacitor (`packaging/`)
